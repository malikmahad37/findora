About FINDORA

FYP System

FINDORA is a modern, responsive, full-stack Lost & Found Management System designed to bridge the gap between people who lose belongings and good samaritans or authorities who find them.

While originally conceived for university environments, FINDORA is engineered as a general-purpose platform suitable for institutions, organizations, business complexes, and municipal communities alike.

Core Design Principles

  • Rule-Based Smart Matching: Transparent, non-black-box algorithmic scoring across keywords, geographical hierarchy, temporal proximity, brands, colors, and classifications.
  • Multi-Tiered Hierarchical Locations: Scalable dynamic schema supporting Country → Province/State → City/District → Local Area/Campus.
  • Ownership Verification: Two-way claim workflow protecting privacy by allowing owners to prove possession before items are returned.
  • Internal Direct Messaging: Keeps user phone numbers and personal emails protected.

Technology Stack

Built with Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy ORM, and modern semantic HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript. Database designed with clear foreign key constraints, capable of running on lightweight SQLite locally or scaling to MySQL in production.
